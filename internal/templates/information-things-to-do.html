Things to do in Santa Fe

Activities

  • Farmer’s market at the Railyard (every Tues & Sat)
  • Railyard Artisan Market (Sat, Sun)
  • Santa Fe Wine & Chile Fiesta (Sept 22–27) — the one-and-only!
  • Get a massage or rent a soaking tub at Ten Thousand Waves
  • Visit the Ski Basin or hike the surrounding Santa Fe National Forest
  • Ride the Sky Train!
  • See a movie at the historic Jean Cocteau Cinema
  • See a show at The Lensic, Meow Wolf, or Santa Fe Brewing
  • Walk the half mile of Canyon Road, which has the highest density of art galleries in the US

Museums

  • New Mexico Museum of Art
  • Site Santa Fe
  • Vladem Contemporary
  • Museum Hill: Indian museum & folk art museum
  • Santa Fe Botanical Garden
  • Georgia O’Keefe Museum

Day trips from Santa Fe

  • Bandelier National Monument
  • The city of Taos & the Taos Pueblo
  • Valles Caldera National Reserve
  • Visit the artisan towns of Madrid and Golden!
