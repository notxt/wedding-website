About us

History

Michael and Adrien met thirteen years ago — in March of 2013 — at a level 1 improv class at the Brody Theater in Portland, Oregon. They were both immediately smitten with each other. But it wasn’t until a year and a half’s worth of classes, performances, and late-night hangouts later that they confessed their feelings one warm September evening. They’ve been inseparable ever since.
