*Travel to Meow Wolf takes approximately ~20m from downtown, depending on traffic. There is ample parking at Meow Wolf.

Parking

Parking in downtown Santa Fe can be LIMITED, especially during evenings and weekends. We recommend arriving downtown 30 minutes before the ceremony so you can find a space.

Garage parking

See the City of Santa Fe parking page for options.

  • Recommended garages: Sandoval Garage, Water Street Municipal Parking, Santa Fe Community Convention Center.
  • The pricing for public lots in downtown Santa Fe is comparable to metered prices.

Metered parking

There is metered parking throughout the downtown area. The easiest way to pay is by downloading the Parkmobile app.
